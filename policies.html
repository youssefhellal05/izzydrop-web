01

Marketplace Terms

Account responsibility

You must provide accurate account and business information, keep your login secure, and only use IzzyDrop for legitimate marketplace activity.

Supplier responsibility

Suppliers are responsible for keeping product information, variants, prices, stock and fulfillment information accurate and for supplying products they are permitted to sell.

Dropshipper responsibility

Dropshippers are responsible for accurate customer information and for the marketing claims, customer communication and selling price they choose outside IzzyDrop.

Marketplace protection

IzzyDrop may restrict products, sourcing activity, integrations or accounts when needed to investigate abuse, inaccurate listings, security issues, payment disputes or marketplace risk.

02

Privacy Notice

IzzyDrop processes information needed to run the marketplace, including account details, business details, product activity, order/customer information, shipping details, settlement records, support messages and technical security information.

Why we use information

To authenticate users, operate products and sourcing, create and fulfill orders, reconcile COD, calculate payouts, provide support, prevent abuse, investigate disputes and maintain marketplace records.

Who may receive it

Information is shared only as needed with the parties involved in the relevant transaction or service, such as the supplier, dropshipper, delivery provider or technical service provider.

Customer data

Customer names, phones, addresses and similar order information must only be used for the relevant order, delivery, support or dispute process.

Your questions

For privacy questions, corrections or account-data requests, contact IzzyDrop support using the email or WhatsApp details on this page.

03

Orders & Cash on Delivery

Order creation

An order is created only through an IzzyDrop order flow. Browsing, showing sourcing interest, linking a sourced product or adding a product to My Products does not create a customer order.

Stock

Stock is reserved by successful order creation. IzzyDrop uses server-side checks to prevent the same available unit from being sold twice.

COD reconciliation

For COD orders, customer collection and courier remittance are separate events. IzzyDrop records the amount expected and the amount actually remitted before payouts are released.

Differences

Zero, partial or disputed remittances remain blocked from normal payout until an authorized reconciliation is recorded with a reason and reference.

04

Returns, Refusals & Refunds

Return handling depends on the actual order state and physical product movement. IzzyDrop does not treat a returned item as sellable stock merely because a refund status changed.

Before delivery

Cancelled or refused orders do not become normal payable settlements. Inventory restoration follows the applicable order/fulfillment state.

After delivery

If a delivered/paid order is later returned and refunded, settlement may enter a reversal process. Already-paid amounts can require recovery, offset or another documented resolution.

Physical return

Returned inventory is restored only when the return is actually received through the supported return-receipt flow. The same return cannot be received twice.

Customer-facing promises

Dropshippers should make customer return promises that they can actually honor. Marketplace settlement handling does not automatically replace the dropshipper’s customer-facing obligations.

05

Supplier & Dropshipper Payouts

When payouts become eligible

Payouts are not released simply because an order exists. IzzyDrop waits for the required delivered/paid settlement state and COD reconciliation.

Evidence

Manual payouts are recorded with a payment method and transaction/reference ID. COD remittance records also require references.

Fees

Applicable marketplace commission, supplier net amount and dropshipper profit are calculated from the order’s recorded purchase prices and displayed in the settlement workflow.

Reversals

If a paid order later becomes refunded or reversed, unresolved payouts remain marked for reversal until each affected party is explicitly resolved with evidence.

06

Sourcing Requests

IzzyDrop sourcing records demand; it is not an order or a promise that a product will be found.

Dropshipper request→Supplier starts sourcing→Supplier obtains and publishes product→Supplier explicitly links it→Sourced offer appears
  • Interest does not reserve stock.
  • Linking a sourced product does not create an order.
  • Linking does not automatically add the product to a dropshipper’s My Products list.
  • Multiple suppliers may source the same community request independently.
  • Supplier cost and suggested retail information come from the supplier’s actual published product.
